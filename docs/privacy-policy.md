# Game Night privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Game Night is a party game app. This policy explains what the app stores, where, and how to delete it.

## The short version

- You can play every game without an account. Nothing about you leaves your phone unless you create one.
- An account only stores your email address, a display name and an emoji avatar.
- There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.
- You can delete your account and its data from inside the app at any time.

## What stays on your phone

The app saves these on your phone only, so it can remember them between game nights:

- your display name and avatar, if you set one without an account
- your settings (sound and haptics, adults-only content on or off)
- game history and the Trivia leaderboard
- team and player names you type into a game

None of this is sent to us. Uninstalling the app removes all of it, and **Profile > Settings > Game history > Clear** removes your game history.

## What an account stores

Accounts are optional. If you create one, the app uses [Supabase](https://supabase.com) to store:

- your email address and a password (the password is stored hashed, never in readable form), used to sign you in
- your display name and emoji avatar, so they follow you to another phone

This data is used only to sign you in and show your profile. Only you can read or change your own profile. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

## Showing Trivia on a TV

If you link a TV, the game's questions, answers and team scores are passed between your phone and the TV through Supabase while you play. They are not saved afterwards.

## Deleting your account and data

**In the app:** open **Profile**, tap **Delete account** and confirm. Your account, email address and profile are deleted straight away and cannot be recovered.

**Without the app:** email the developer at the contact address shown on Game Night's Google Play page, from the email address on your account, and ask for your account to be deleted. We will delete the account and all its data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Data kept only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app.

## Children

Game Night is meant for groups of friends and family and is not directed at children under 13. Adults-only (18+) cards are off by default and only appear when someone switches them on in Profile. We do not knowingly create accounts for children under 13; if you believe a child has created one, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: use the developer email address on Game Night's Google Play page.
